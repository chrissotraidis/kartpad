All four runtime PRs are merged. They carry the six-bit selector correction. Mac also carries the two
controller fixes. [Renderer evidence](artifacts/2026-10-29/matrix-selector.md)
records 24 actual GPU cases across three Android-source rendering paths, eight
portable counterfactual failures, four-pin sanitizer passes and old-cache reuse.
The affected S24 draw samples do not exercise this defect.

[Mobile menu and short-session evidence](artifacts/2026-10-29/mobile-bloom-and-session.md)
records the earlier incremental Android run, whose runtime/game pack predates
these pins. The subsequent normal Android Classic/Beta and PGO pack builds,
normal iOS app build, and clean Mac/iOS PadMint player builds passed with the
new pins. No full native tvOS build was performed in this pass.

The [iOS record](artifacts/2026-10-29/integrated-ios.md) distinguishes the
audit-passing no-game-code base IPA from the private personal IPA, which is not
publishable. The [Mac record](artifacts/2026-10-29/integrated-macos.md) covers
driven Original/Retro race smoke checks in an isolated profile. The
[stale Mac icon](artifacts/2026-10-29/macos-icon.md) is corrected; an icon-only
repack passed package and signature checks. The
[Android record](artifacts/2026-10-29/integrated-android.md) covers passing
fresh/update imports, Original/Retro Classic/Beta race gates, and the complete
repeated-course and mixed-course/restart memory comparisons. These checks do
not establish physical-device acceptance or authorize a release.

The [extended character-renderer investigation](artifacts/2026-10-29/character-renderer.md)
adds five actual GPU fixtures across five supported Metal paths and compares
three exact reported pipeline recipes with synthetic inputs. All pass locally.
The regression tests are merged in [runtime #14](https://github.com/chrissotraidis/wiicompiled/pull/14)
without changing the integrated app pin. No failing Vulkan draw or affected
phone was reproduced, and no missing-character fix is claimed. The owner
confirmed no affected phone is available and requested continued local validation.

[Ghost draft #375](https://github.com/chrissotraidis/kartpad/pull/375) is updated
separately at `1d2c7515`; the merge conflict is resolved and four hosted checks
pass. Its wider native replay and Apple acceptance gates are retained. #522's
WFC-host guard and Android beta resolution correction are already merged after
0.8.6 and belong in release integration.

No entire open issue gained new end-to-end acceptance sufficient for closure in
this pass. Replies report concrete code/test outcomes; unchanged larger requests
do not get routine status comments. No version bump or release was published.
