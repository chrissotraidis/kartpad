KartPad 0.8.13 (build 279) fixes the Retro Rewind crash after a Grand Prix for players who use Retro Rewind in a language other than English, puts the speed settings into one **Speed** choice on Android, iPhone and iPad, decodes the menu movies natively so cup and course select keep up better on slower phones, and helps Snapdragon players on Qualcomm's 512.762 driver get a Turnip driver from inside the app.

### Fixed

- **Retro Rewind crash after a Grand Prix:** with Retro Rewind set to any language except English, the game stopped with *ARCInitHandle: bad archive format* when the trophy ceremony or the ending began. Retro Rewind switches the award screen to files for the chosen language, but its list of files leaves those out, so the game got an empty archive. KartPad now supplies the award files Retro Rewind ships for each language. Checked in Spanish, with both the trophy ceremony and the ending for a low finish, on the Android emulator. The same fix is in the iPhone, iPad and Mac runtimes. ([#131](https://github.com/chrissotraidis/kartpad/issues/131), [#493](https://github.com/chrissotraidis/kartpad/issues/493))

### Added

- **Speed (Android, iPhone and iPad):** **⋯ → Speed & Graphics → Speed** sets the two speed switches in one choice. **Smoothest** turns on 30 FPS Races and Simpler Computer Karts, for devices that slow down in races. **Balanced** turns on only Simpler Computer Karts. **Best Quality**, the default, turns both off. You can still change each switch yourself, and Speed then shows **Custom**. Your 0.8.12 switches stay as they were, and Speed shows the preset they match.
- **Slow-race offer (Android):** after a race (not a Time Trial) that ran mostly below 50 FPS with 30 FPS Races off, KartPad says how fast the race ran and offers once to switch to Smoothest.
- **Turnip offer on the 512.762 driver (Android):** on Adreno 6xx and 7xx phones with Qualcomm's 512.762 driver, which leaves characters missing or white, KartPad shows **Recommended for This Phone** once, a few seconds after the game first starts, with **Get a Driver…**. It doesn't appear when a custom driver is already chosen. ([#104](https://github.com/chrissotraidis/kartpad/issues/104))
- **Mr. Purple's Turnip (Android):** **GPU Driver → Add Driver… → Download a Driver…** lists Mr. Purple's Turnip first. Recommended for This Phone names the builds players confirmed: Mr. Purple's Turnip T30 on Adreno 7xx (Galaxy S24 Ultra) and a K11MCH1 Turnip on Adreno 6xx (Moto G85). ([#104](https://github.com/chrissotraidis/kartpad/issues/104), [#301](https://github.com/chrissotraidis/kartpad/issues/301))

### Changed

- **Menu movies decode natively:** the moving previews in the menus are decoded by KartPad's own code. On the Android emulator the video thread's time per frame fell by about 43% at the main menu and Select Cup. In a test of all 27 movies on the disc, every frame matched the reference decoders it was compared with. How much this helps on a phone isn't measured yet. ([#584](https://github.com/chrissotraidis/kartpad/issues/584))
- **Speed & Graphics:** **⋯ → Graphics & Performance** is now **⋯ → Speed & Graphics** and comes before Display. On Android the rows only Android has (Graphics Engine, 120 FPS Races, GPU Driver and Graphics Diagnostics) follow the ones iPhone and iPad share.
- **The graphics engines say Vulkan (Android):** the choices read **Classic (Vulkan)** and **WiiCompiled 0.9 Beta (Vulkan)**, because both already draw with Vulkan. ([#576](https://github.com/chrissotraidis/kartpad/issues/576))
- **120 FPS Races only with the Beta engine (Android):** on Classic the extra frames made races stutter, so the switch shows only with the WiiCompiled 0.9 Beta engine. Your choice stays saved for when you switch to Beta.
- **Diagnostics for slow races with touch auto-accelerate (Android):** the diagnostic log records how long since the screen was last touched, whether auto-accelerate is holding A, and the processor's clock speeds, to find out why some phones slow down when no finger is on the screen. ([#554](https://github.com/chrissotraidis/kartpad/issues/554))

### Where things moved

| --- | --- |
| ⋯ → Graphics & Performance, after Display | ⋯ → **Speed & Graphics**, before Display |
| — | **Speed**, first in Speed & Graphics |
| Graphics Engine, second in Graphics & Performance (Android) | After Simpler Computer Karts (Android) |
| *Classic* and *WiiCompiled 0.9 (Beta)* | *Classic (Vulkan)* and *WiiCompiled 0.9 Beta (Vulkan)* |

### Updating

- **Android:** tap **Update available → Update Now**, or download the APK below and install it over 0.8.12. Don't uninstall first. Your game data, saves, Retro Rewind and ghosts stay.
- **iPhone and iPad:** build 0.8.13 with the latest [PadMint](https://github.com/chrissotraidis/padmint/releases/latest) and install it over your current KartPad with the same sideloading tool and Apple ID.
- **Apple Silicon Mac:** rebuild 0.8.13 with PadMint and replace only the app; your data stays.
