KartPad 0.7.15 updates Retro Rewind to 6.13.1 and brings fixes for saves, ghosts and Apple controls.

> **Draft:** final gameplay and release checks remain. This release is not published.

| You want KartPad on | How |
|---|---|
| **Android** | `KartPad-v0.7.15-android.apk` |
| **iPhone, iPad and Mac** | Build it with [PadMint](https://github.com/chrissotraidis/padmint) |

Full steps: **[Get KartPad](https://github.com/chrissotraidis/kartpad#get-kartpad)**.

### What's new

- **Retro Rewind 6.13.1.** KartPad downloads and applies the matching official pack and update. On iPhone and iPad, replacing an older pack now preserves its saves and ghosts.
- **Android: recover from a failed ghost import.** On the game chooser, open **Help → Cancel Pending Ghost Import** to clear the pending request without removing your saves. Save and ghost writes also handle failures more reliably.
- **Original Mario Kart Wii: compressed ghost repair.** Fixes compressed ghost handling. Retro Rewind ghost transfer is still separate work.
- **iPhone and iPad: music and game sounds, separately.** Sound controls let you adjust each, with settings kept when you leave and reopen the app.
- **Apple motion controls.** Corrects steering when the device is held flat while continuing to detect shake gestures.
- **PadMint on Mac: first-time setup.** Fetches required dependencies instead of stopping when they are not already downloaded.

### Updating

- **Android:** install the APK over your current KartPad; don't uninstall first. Your saves and game data stay.
- **iPhone, iPad and Mac:** build 0.7.15 with PadMint. On iPhone and iPad, install over your current KartPad with the same sideloading tool, Apple ID and bundle ID to keep your saves.
- Open **Retro Rewind** and complete its official pack update before playing.

**You still need your own Mario Kart Wii** (PAL, RMCP01, revision 0). No disc images, extracted game files or keys are included.

### Checked so far

- The signed Android APK installs over 0.7.14 with existing app data unchanged and reaches a race. A fresh installation imports game data and also reaches a race.
- The same APK installs Retro Rewind 6.13.1 and reaches a Retro race with working acceleration.
- The iPad candidate installs in place with checked saves and settings unchanged. The iPad simulator completes the Retro pack upgrade while retaining a test save.
- `KartPad-v0.7.15-ios-for-padmint.ipa` is the iPhone app without game code, for PadMint.

Completed races/results, production-online play, physical Apple checks and a fresh Mac build remain unverified.
