KartPad 0.7.15 updates Retro Rewind to 6.13.1, so Retro WFC online works again, and brings fixes for saves, ghosts and Apple controls.

| You want KartPad on | How |
|---|---|
| **Android** | `KartPad-v0.7.15-android.apk` |
| **iPhone, iPad and Mac** | Build it with [PadMint](https://github.com/chrissotraidis/padmint) |

Full steps: **[Get KartPad](https://github.com/chrissotraidis/kartpad#get-kartpad)**.

### What's new

- **Retro Rewind 6.13.1.** KartPad downloads and applies the matching official pack and update, which Retro WFC requires for online play. On iPhone and iPad, replacing an older pack now keeps its saves and ghosts.
- **Android: recover from a failed ghost import.** On the game chooser, open **Help → Cancel Pending Ghost Import** to clear the pending request without removing your saves. Save and ghost writes also handle failures more reliably.
- **Compressed ghosts in Original Mario Kart Wii.** Ghosts from sites such as Chadsoft (compressed `.rkg` files) now import in a form the game can replay. Retro Rewind ghost transfer is still separate work.
- **iPhone and iPad: music and game sounds, separately.** Sound controls let you adjust each, with settings kept when you leave and reopen the app.
- **Apple motion controls.** Steering returns to center when the device is held flat, and shake tricks keep working.
- **PadMint on Mac.** A first Mac build now fetches everything it needs, including Retro Rewind and the translator source, instead of stopping partway.

### Updating

- **Android:** tap **Update available → Update Now** on the game chooser, or install the APK over your current KartPad; don't uninstall first. Your saves and game data stay.
- **iPhone, iPad and Mac:** build 0.7.15 with PadMint. On iPhone and iPad, install over your current KartPad with the same sideloading tool and Apple ID to keep your saves.
- Open **Retro Rewind** and let its official pack update finish before playing online.

**You still need your own Mario Kart Wii** (PAL, RMCP01, revision 0). No disc images, game files or keys are included.

### Checked before release

- Android emulator (Android 16): this APK installs over 0.7.14 with the save and game data unchanged and reaches a race; a fresh install imports game data and reaches a race.
- The same APK installs Retro Rewind 6.13.1, reaches a Retro race, signs in to Retro WFC, joins a worldwide room and races other players online. On the emulator the room dropped twice mid-race; each time KartPad returned to the menu and signed in again. This is listed in known issues while we check phones.
- A compressed Luigi Circuit ghost from Chadsoft imports, appears in Time Trials and replays around the track.
- iPad: the iPhone/iPad app installs in place with saves and settings unchanged. The iPad Simulator upgrades an older Retro Rewind pack to 6.13.1 and keeps the save.
- Mac: a clean PadMint build from this release's source completes.
- The sound and motion fixes pass automated tests; they have not yet had hands-on testing on an iPhone or iPad.
- `KartPad-v0.7.15-ios-for-padmint.ipa` is the iPhone app without game code, for PadMint.
