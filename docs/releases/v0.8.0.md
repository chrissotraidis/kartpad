KartPad 0.8.0 moves to the latest WiiCompiled engine, tells iPhone, iPad and Mac players when an update is out, and adds Shake to Trick and a choice of app icons.

| You want KartPad on | How |
|---|---|
| **Android** | `KartPad-v0.8.0-android.apk` |
| **iPhone, iPad and Mac** | Build it with [PadMint](https://github.com/chrissotraidis/padmint) |

Full steps: **[Get KartPad](https://github.com/chrissotraidis/kartpad#get-kartpad)**.

### What's new

- **Latest WiiCompiled engine.** Includes the fix for 3D models and tracks that could disappear while the HUD kept drawing, and cleaner recovery when an online connection fails partway through sending.
- **Update notices on iPhone, iPad and Mac.** When a new KartPad is out, **Update available** appears next to **Help** on the iPhone/iPad game chooser. On Mac, use **Help → Check for Updates…**. Build the update with PadMint as before; iPhone and iPad can't install it by themselves.
- **Shake to Trick.** **Controls → Shake to Trick…** on Android, iPhone and iPad: shake the device to do a trick or wheelie. It's off by default and works without tilt steering.
- **Choose the app icon.** **Display → App Icon** offers the red K, Circuit and Mono. On Android, KartPad closes to apply the new icon.
- **Android: Fill Screen draws races under the display cutout**, which may remove the black band some handhelds, such as the AYN Thor, showed.
- **PadMint:** build the iPhone/iPad app on an Intel Mac (experimental), and build the Mac app without installing Homebrew tools. Both need PadMint 0.4.12.

### Updating

- **Android:** tap **Update available → Update Now** on the game chooser, or install the APK over your current KartPad; don't uninstall first. Your saves and game data stay.
- **iPhone, iPad and Mac:** build 0.8.0 with PadMint. On iPhone and iPad, install over your current KartPad with the same sideloading tool and Apple ID to keep your saves. This version needs a new game pack, which PadMint builds for you.

**You still need your own Mario Kart Wii** (PAL, RMCP01, revision 0). No disc images, game files or keys are included.

### Checked before release

- Android emulator (Android 16): this APK installs over 0.7.15 with all 2,079 app data files unchanged and reaches a race; a fresh install imports game data and reaches a race. Switching the app icon and back works.
- Pixel 9 Pro XL: updated in place with saves and licences kept; a race in Original at 60 FPS; Retro Rewind 6.13.1 downloaded and installed in the app, started, and signed in to Retro WFC.
- iPad Pro: the iPhone/iPad app installs in place with saves and settings unchanged and runs Original at 60 FPS. The app icon choices are registered for iPhone and iPad.
- Mac: a full PadMint build with only the system's own tools on the path completes, and the game runs at 60 FPS. The update check reaches GitHub.
- Shake to Trick and the icon picker have automated and emulator checks; they have not yet had hands-on testing on a phone or iPad.
- `KartPad-v0.8.0-ios-for-padmint.ipa` is the iPhone app without game code, for PadMint.
